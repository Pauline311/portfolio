Next Step

Next Step is a career path guide app designed for Grade 12 and early college students. It helps learners explore career recommendations, understand IT career descriptions, take interactive quizzes, and access certification guides. The app also includes a bookmarking feature for easy reference. Built with Java/Kotlinand it follows modern Material Design principles.

  • Career recommendations based on interests
  • IT career descriptions and pathways
  • Interactive quizzes
  • Certification guide for students
  • Bookmarking for saved careers
